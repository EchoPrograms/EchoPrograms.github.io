I am dedicated to learning technical skills for myself. I take care to learn skills and complete learning projects myself, using AI as a tool to find information or help me get unstuck.

This site is a practical project with a defined end goal. It is not intended to teach me frontend development, and it is not a critical system. For this kind of work, I can effectively use AI agents alongside my own skills to reach the result I want.

Writeups for projects completed before this site may be generated by AI from documents I provide. Those writeups are labeled AI Generated Writeups Imported from Chat History and reviewed for accuracy. This lets me communicate the scope of my abilities and experience without turning the process of documenting older work into an unnecessarily extensive task.

The AI use policy provides a more detailed account of how AI contributed to this website.
